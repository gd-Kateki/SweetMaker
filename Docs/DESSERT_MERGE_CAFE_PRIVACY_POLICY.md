# Privacy Policy — Dessert Merge Cafe

**Last updated: 6 November 2026**  
**Developer and publisher: OffCut Studios**  
**Contact: studiosoffcut@gmail.com**

OffCut Studios ("we", "us", or "our") develops and publishes Dessert Merge Cafe
(the "Game") for Android. This Privacy Policy explains how information is
collected, used, disclosed, stored, and deleted when you use the Game, its online
features, and our player-support services.

## 1. Information we process

### Account and sign-in information

When you sign in through Google Play Games, Google and Unity Authentication
process account identifiers and authentication information needed to connect you
to your game account. These can include your Google Play Games player identifier,
Unity player identifier, and authentication/session tokens. We do not receive
your Google account password.

### Gameplay progress and cloud backups

The Game stores progress locally on your device. When your account is signed in
and cloud saving is available, the Game automatically uploads eligible progress
to Unity Cloud Save. This information includes your cafe name, currency balance,
lifetime earnings and completed-order totals, rebirth progress, upgrades, board
and shelf contents, customer orders, dessert collection, tutorial progress,
energy state, and information needed to validate and restore a save.

Cloud records also contain account identifiers, save checksums and synchronization
information needed to associate progress with the correct player and manage
conflicting saves. The Game uses Unity Cloud Code to process saved totals for
leaderboard publication.

### Public leaderboard information

The Game publishes your cafe name, lifetime money earned, lifetime orders
completed, current rebirth, and ranking to Unity Leaderboards. Other signed-in
players can view the cafe name, relevant total, rebirth, and rank for each entry.
Leaderboard records are associated with a Unity player identifier.

Please choose a cafe name you are comfortable making public. Do not include your
real name, email address, phone number, or other personal information in it.
Your full gameplay backup and authentication tokens are not displayed in the
leaderboard interface.

### Device, connection, and diagnostic information

The Game's service providers may process information such as IP address,
installation or device identifiers, device model, operating system, app version,
language, network information, request timestamps, and error or diagnostic
information. The information processed depends on the service, device settings,
and privacy choices that apply.

### Optional analytics

The Game includes Unity Analytics and a separate analytics consent choice.
If you enable analytics, Unity may process installation identifiers, technical
device information, application lifecycle events, session information, and IP
address to help us understand use of the Game and improve its operation.

You can change your analytics choice in the Game's Settings. Declining optional
analytics does not prevent normal gameplay. Withdrawing consent stops future
analytics collection through the Game's analytics consent controls; it does not
automatically delete information already received by Unity or cancel an upload
already in progress. You can request deletion of previously collected information
as described below.

### Advertising information

The Game displays advertisements through Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads. Ads
include optional rewarded advertisements for energy or cash bonuses, and
interstitial advertisements that may appear when returning to the home screen.

Advertising services may process Android Advertising ID when available, other
device or installation identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred
from IP address, device and network information, ad requests, ad views, clicks,
reward-completion events, and diagnostic information. These services use data
for advertising delivery, measurement, frequency management, security, fraud
prevention, and, where applicable, personalization.

Advertising services can process information when their SDK initializes or
requests an advertisement, even if you do not choose to watch a rewarded ad.
The analytics consent setting does not control advertising data processing.
Unity and its advertising partners describe their processing and available
choices in the policies linked in Section 4.

### Support and privacy requests

If you contact us, we receive the email address you use, your message, and any
information you choose to provide, such as a game identifier or diagnostic
details. Do not send passwords, authentication tokens, payment-card information,
or identity documents when contacting support.

## 2. Why information is used

We and the relevant service providers process information to:

- Operate the Game and preserve gameplay progress.
- Authenticate players and associate cloud progress with the correct account.
- Back up and restore progress, including after reinstalling the Game.
- Publish and display leaderboard rankings.
- Deliver and measure advertising and grant earned ad rewards.
- Understand app use through optional analytics when enabled.
- Diagnose errors, secure services, and prevent abuse or fraud.
- Respond to support, privacy, and deletion requests.
- Meet legal obligations and resolve disputes.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, processing may be necessary to
provide a requested service, pursue legitimate interests such as security and
support, comply with legal obligations, or act on consent. Processing that legally
requires consent must be based on valid consent; this policy is not itself a
request for, or evidence of, that consent.

## 3. Local storage, cloud storage, and connectivity

Local saves and cloud backups are separate. Some gameplay can continue without
a connection after the Game is ready, but sign-in, cloud synchronization,
leaderboards, and advertising depend on network and service availability.

Clearing Android app storage or uninstalling the Game can remove local progress.
Those actions do not by themselves delete cloud backups, authentication records,
leaderboard entries, or information already held by service providers. Signing
out also does not delete those records.

## 5. Your choices

- Review and change optional analytics consent in the Game's Settings.
- Use Android's privacy or advertising settings to manage or delete your
  Advertising ID where supported. The available settings depend on Android and
  Google Play services versions. This does not necessarily stop all advertising
  or all processing needed to deliver contextual ads.
- Use privacy options provided by Unity or within advertisements where available.
- Avoid personal information in your public cafe name.
- Contact us to request access, correction, account deletion, or data deletion.

Advertising-related disclosures or transfers may qualify as a "sale", "sharing",
or targeted advertising under some regional laws, even when no money is paid for
the information. Where those laws apply, you may exercise the relevant opt-out
rights through available provider controls and by contacting us. Unity's privacy
choices apply to its services; they do not automatically govern every other
provider's processing.

## 6. Retention

Local progress remains on your device until it is removed, overwritten, or reset.
We retain account-linked cloud progress and leaderboard information for as long
as needed to provide the corresponding features, unless you request deletion or
another deletion requirement applies.

Support correspondence and request records are retained only as needed to handle
the request, maintain appropriate operational records, resolve disputes, or meet
legal obligations. Service providers may maintain their own retention schedules
for information they process under their own policies.

Deletion can require additional processing time for provider systems and backup
copies. If particular information must be retained for legal, security, or fraud
prevention reasons, we limit it to the necessary purpose and explain applicable
exceptions in responding to your request.

## 7. Account and data deletion

You can request deletion without reinstalling the Game by emailing
**studiosoffcut@gmail.com**, preferably with the subject
**Dessert Merge Cafe — Account and Data Deletion**.

Tell us whether you want to delete your game account and associated data, specific
cloud progress, leaderboard information, or previously collected analytics data.
Provide a game identifier if available. If you cannot access the Game or find an
identifier, contact us for assistance. We may request additional non-sensitive
information to locate the records and verify that you are authorized to request
their deletion; a public identifier alone may not establish account ownership.

For an account-deletion request, we will arrange deletion of the associated game
account and relevant cloud progress, summary records, and leaderboard entries,
and coordinate applicable provider deletion requests. We will respond without
undue delay and in accordance with applicable law, including explaining any
permitted retention exceptions. Deletion can be irreversible and may prevent
recovery of your progress.

Game-account deletion does not delete your Google account or Google Play Games
profile. Manage those accounts through Google. Locally retained progress may
remain on your device; clear app storage if you also want to remove that copy.
Continuing to use the Game or signing in again can create new records.

Further instructions are available on our
[Data Deletion Requests page](https://offcut-studios.github.io/offcutstudios-legal/dessert-merge-cafe/data-deletion/).

If you believe a child has provided information inappropriately, contact us at
**studiosoffcut@gmail.com** so we can investigate and take appropriate action,
including deletion where required. This statement does not replace any age or

Unity, Google, and other relevant providers may process information outside your
country, including in countries with different data-protection laws. Applicable
transfer safeguards depend on the recipient and service; consult the linked
provider policies for their international-processing arrangements.
