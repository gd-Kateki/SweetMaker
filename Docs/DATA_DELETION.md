# Account and Data Deletion — Dessert Merge Cafe

**Developer: OffCut Studios**  
**Last updated: 6 November 2026**

You can request deletion of your Dessert Merge Cafe game account and associated
data without reinstalling the Game.

## Submit a request

Email **studiosoffcut@gmail.com** with the subject
**Dessert Merge Cafe — Account and Data Deletion**.

Tell us what you want deleted. A complete game-account request covers the Unity
Authentication game account, Cloud Save backups and summary fields, leaderboard
entries, and applicable analytics data. You can also ask about deleting specific
records. Include your game player identifier if available. If you cannot find it
or no longer have the Game installed, contact us and we will help locate the
records. Never send a Google password, authentication token or identity document.

In the Game, **Settings → Request account / data deletion** opens instructions
and can prepare a request email with your available player identifier. Review
and send that email. Merely opening a screen or email composer does not submit
a request or delete anything.

[Prepare a deletion-request email](mailto:studiosoffcut@gmail.com?subject=Dessert%20Merge%20Cafe%20-%20Account%20and%20Data%20Deletion)

## Verification and completion

We verify your authority to request deletion before acting. Knowledge of a public
player identifier alone may not establish ownership. We will respond without
undue delay in accordance with applicable law, explain any further information
needed, arrange applicable provider deletion requests and confirm the outcome.

Deleted progress may be unrecoverable. Deleting your game account does not delete
your Google account or Google Play Games profile.

## Local and cloud data are separate

Uninstalling the Game, clearing Android app storage or signing out does not by
itself delete cloud records. Deleting cloud records does not remotely clear your
device. To remove local progress, use Android Settings → Apps → Dessert Merge
Cafe → Storage → Clear storage. This removes local progress and privacy choices.

After requesting account deletion, avoid continuing to play or signing in again
until support confirms the outcome; using the Game again can generate new data.

## Retention exceptions

We may retain limited information when necessary for legal obligations,
security/fraud prevention or dispute handling, including an appropriate record
that a request was fulfilled. We will explain relevant exceptions. Provider
systems and backups can require additional processing time. Optional analytics
withdrawal stops future collection but does not itself erase past data.
